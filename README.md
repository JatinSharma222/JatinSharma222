hey, i'm jatin. i build software that does more than just respond to requests — from full-stack products and ai agents to the infrastructure that keeps them running.
 
i work across **typescript**, **rust**, **python** and **c++**, building with **react**, **next.js**, **node.js** and **bun**. lately, i've been interested in agentic systems — giving llms tools, connecting them to real apis and infrastructure, and building workflows that can reason, take actions, and get things done beyond a chat interface.
 
i enjoy working close to the metal, too: distributed systems, backend architecture, devops and performance engineering. **postgres**, **redis**, **docker**, **github actions** — the unglamorous parts that make software reliable at scale. on the ai side, i work with **tensorflow** and machine learning; on web3, i'm exploring **solana** and **anchor**.
 
i'm especially drawn to the intersection of ai agents and systems engineering: how agents use tools, interact with real-world systems, coordinate workflows, and operate reliably outside a demo. i care about understanding what's happening under the hood, writing maintainable code, and building things that solve actual problems.
 
less hype, more systems that work.
